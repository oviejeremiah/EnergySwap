Sign-in wallet Not connected

Your Hedera account is your trading identity, verifiable on HashScan. Your sign-in wallet is separate and holds no HBAR.

About the energy amounts

The kWh amount in an offer is provided by the prosumer. You do not need your own smart meter to trade. A meter gateway can report generated energy for a defined time period and create a record both parties can inspect. In this prototype, meter readings are not linked to individual offers or trades. A record shows what the gateway reported; it does not independently prove that energy was physically delivered.
